Privacy

What leaves your browser

Nothing you type or paste in the editor is ever sent anywhere. The document is converted to a PDF by your browser, on your device. There is no server that receives your Markdown, because there is no server.

What is stored

Nothing. The app uses no cookies, no local storage and no session storage for your content. Page setup choices live in memory only and are forgotten when you close the tab.

Accounts, uploads, payment

There are no accounts, no uploads and no payment. The tool opens, it works, it closes.

Analytics

If a Cloudflare Web Analytics token is configured at build time, the page loads a cookie-less counter of roughly 11 KB. It records page views and referrers. It never sees your document. If no token is configured, no analytics script is loaded at all.
